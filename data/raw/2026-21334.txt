ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA proposes to supersede Airworthiness Directive (AD) 
2025-09-07, which applies to certain Airbus SAS Model A330-243, -243F, 
-841, and -941 airplanes. AD 2025-09-07 requires revising the existing 
airplane flight manual (AFM) by providing instructions to address dual 
loss of radio management panel (RMP) data synchronization, and the 
existing minimum equipment list (MEL) by removing relief for an 
inoperative RMP 3. Since the FAA issued AD 2025-09-07, a modification 
that addresses the unsafe condition has been developed. This proposed 
AD would continue to require the actions specified in AD 2025-09-07 and 
would require modifying the airplane. This proposed AD also reduces the 
applicability. This proposed AD would also prohibit the installation of 
affected parts under certain conditions. The FAA is proposing this AD 
to address the unsafe condition on these products.

Background

    The FAA issued AD 2025-09-07, Amendment 39-23028 (90 FR 17888, 
April 30, 2025) (AD 2025-09-07), for

[[Page 66017]]

certain Airbus SAS Model A330-243, -243F, -841, and -941 airplanes. AD 
2025-09-07 was prompted by an MCAI originated by EASA, which is the 
Technical Agent for the Member States of the European Union. EASA 
issued EASA AD 2025-0043, dated February 19, 2025 (EASA AD 2025-0043), 
to correct an unsafe condition. EASA later revised EASA AD 2025-0043 
and issued EASA AD 2025-0043R1, dated August 7, 2025 (EASA AD 2025-
0043R1) to reduce the applicability by removing airplanes that are not 
affected by the unsafe condition.
    AD 2025-09-07 requires revising the existing AFM by providing 
instructions to address dual loss of RMP data synchronization, and the 
existing MEL by removing relief for an inoperative RMP 3. The FAA 
issued AD 2025-09-07 to address loss of radio communication, 
uncommanded changes of transponder and traffic alert and collision 
avoidance system (TCAS) settings, and activation of standby navigation 
mode on all RMPs. This condition, if not corrected, could result in 
total loss of radio communication if RMP 3 is inoperative prior to 
dispatch, including loss of transponder functionality and standby 
navigation. The FAA considered AD 2025-09-07 to be an interim action 
and stated that, once a modification was developed, approved, and 
available, the FAA might consider additional rulemaking. The 
manufacturer has since developed a modification that would terminate 
the actions required by AD 2025-09-07. The FAA has determined that this 
modification must be required.

Actions Since AD 2025-09-07 Was Issued

    Since the FAA issued AD 2025-09-07, EASA superseded EASA AD 2025-
0043, dated February 19, 2025, and issued EASA AD 2025-0286, dated 
December 17, 2025 (EASA AD 2025-0286) (also referred to as the MCAI), 
to correct an unsafe condition on certain Airbus SAS Model A330-941 
airplanes. The MCAI states that occurrences were reported of loss of 
data synchronization between the RMPs and the audio management unit, 
which can result in the loss of control of radio communication, 
uncommanded changes of transponder and TCAS settings, and the 
activation of standby navigation mode on all RMPs. This condition, if 
not corrected, could lead to total loss of control of radio 
communications, including the loss of communications, transponder 
functionality, standby navigation, or any combination of these. To 
address this condition, Airbus designed and certified modification 
211442 (install wiring and software L4.3 standard for digital audio) 
and published service information that provides instructions for 
installing that modification on in-service airplanes; EASA AD 2025-0286 
retains the AFM and MEL requirements of EASA AD 2025-0043R1 and adds a 
requirement to modify the airplane.
    This proposed AD would reduce the applicability and only apply to 
certain Model A330-941 airplanes. Airbus SAS Model A330-243, -243F, and 
-841 airplanes are not affected by the identified unsafe condition. The 
FAA is proposing this AD to address the unsafe condition on these 
products.
    You may examine the MCAI in the AD docket at regulations.gov under 
Docket No. FAA-2026-11813.

Material Incorporated by Reference Under 1 CFR Part 51

    EASA AD 2025-0286 specifies procedures for modifying the airplane 
by installing wiring and software (the digital radio and audio 
integrating management system (DRAIMS) L4.3 configuration). EASA AD 
2025-0286 also specifies revising the AFM and the MEL and, after the 
modification is accomplished, prohibits installing affected parts.
    This material is reasonably available because the interested 
parties have access to it through their normal course of business or by

FAA's Determination

    These products have been approved by the civil aviation authority 
of another country and are approved for operation in the United States. 
Pursuant to the FAA's bilateral agreement with this State of Design 
Authority, that authority has notified the FAA of the unsafe condition 
described in the MCAI referenced above. The FAA is issuing this NPRM 
after determining that the unsafe condition described previously is 
likely to exist or develop on other products of the same type design.

Proposed AD Requirements in This NPRM

    This proposed AD would retain all requirements of AD 2025-09-07 and 
reduce the applicability of AD 2025-09-07. This proposed AD would also 
require accomplishing the modification specified in EASA AD 2025-0286 
described previously, except for any differences identified as 
exceptions in the regulatory text of this proposed AD. Accomplishing 
that modification would terminate the retained AFM and MEL revision 
requirements. This proposed AD would also prohibit the installation of 
affected parts under certain conditions. See ``Differences Between This 
Proposed AD and the MCAI'' for a discussion of the general differences 
included in this AD.

Differences Between This Proposed AD and the MCAI

    This proposed AD does not adopt paragraphs (1), (2), and (3) of 
EASA AD 2025-0286 (EASA's retained AFM and master minimum equipment 
list requirements). Instead, this proposed AD retains and restates the 
corresponding AFM and MEL revision requirements of AD 2025-09-07 in 
paragraphs (g) and (h) of this AD.

Explanation of Required Compliance Information

    In the FAA's ongoing efforts to improve the efficiency of the AD 
process, the FAA developed a process to use some civil aviation 
authority (CAA) ADs as the primary source of information for compliance 
with requirements for corresponding FAA ADs. The FAA has been 
coordinating this process with manufacturers and CAAs. As a result, the 
FAA proposes to incorporate EASA AD 2025-0286 by reference in the FAA 
final rule. This proposed AD would, therefore, require compliance with 
EASA AD 2025-0286 in its entirety through that incorporation, except 
for any differences identified as exceptions in the regulatory text of 
this proposed AD. Using common terms that are the same as the heading 
of a particular section in EASA AD 2025-0286 does not mean that 
operators need comply only with that section. For example, where the AD 
requirement refers to ``all required actions and compliance times,'' 
compliance with this AD requirement is not limited to the section 
titled ``Required Action(s) and Compliance Time(s)'' in EASA AD 2025-
0286. Material required by EASA AD 2025-0286 is available at 
regulations.gov by searching for and locating Docket No. FAA-2026-11813 
after the FAA final rule is published.

Authority for This Rulemaking

    Title 49 of the United States Code specifies the FAA's authority to 
issue rules on aviation safety. Subtitle I, section 106, describes the 
authority of the FAA Administrator. Subtitle VII: Aviation Programs, 
describes in more detail the scope of the Agency's authority.
    The FAA is issuing this rulemaking under the authority described in 
Subtitle VII, Part A, Subpart III, Section 44701: General requirements. 
Under that section, Congress charges the FAA with promoting safe flight 
of civil aircraft in air commerce by prescribing regulations for 
practices, methods, and procedures the Administrator finds necessary 
for safety in air commerce. This regulation is within the scope of that 
authority because it addresses an unsafe condition that is likely to 
exist or develop on products identified in this rulemaking action.

(a) Comments Due Date

    The FAA must receive comments on this airworthiness directive 
(AD) by December 3, 2026.

(b) Affected ADs

    This AD replaces AD 2025-09-07, Amendment 39-23028 (90 FR 17888, 
April 30, 2025) (AD 2025-09-07).

(c) Applicability

    This AD applies to Airbus SAS Model A330-941 airplanes, 
certificated in any category, as identified in European Union 
Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) AD 2025-0286, dated December 17, 2025 
(EASA AD 2025-0286).

(d) Subject

    Air Transport Association (ATA) of America Code 23, 
Communications.

(e) Unsafe Condition

    This AD was prompted by reports of loss of data synchronization 
between the radio management panels (RMPs) and the audio management 
unit. The FAA is issuing this AD to address such loss of data 
synchronization, which can result in the loss of control of radio 
communication, uncommanded changes of transponder and Traffic Alert 
and Collision Avoidance System (TCAS) settings, and the activation 
of standby navigation mode on all RMPs. The unsafe condition, if not 
addressed, could result in the total loss of radio communications if 
RMP 3 is inoperative prior to dispatch, including the loss of 
transponder functionality and standby navigation.

(f) Compliance

    Comply with this AD within the compliance times specified, 
unless already done.

(g) Retained Airplane Flight Manual (AFM) Revision, With No Changes

    This paragraph restates the requirements of paragraph (g) of AD 
2025-09-07, with no changes. Within 7 days after May 15, 2025 (the 
effective date of AD 2025-09-07), revise the Emergency Procedures 
section of the existing AFM to include the information in figure 1 
to paragraph (g) of this AD. This may be done by inserting a copy of 
figure 1 to paragraph (g) of this AD into the existing AFM. Using a 
different document with information identical to that contained in 
figure 1 to paragraph (g) of this AD is acceptable for compliance 
with the requirements of this paragraph.

                Figure 1 to Paragraph (g)--AFM Procedure
------------------------------------------------------------------------
                  Dual loss of RMP data synchronization
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Switch off RMP 1 and RMP 2.
Note: When RMP 1 and RMP 2 are switched off:
    --The aural alerts are not available on loudspeakers.
    --The communication is only available on RMP 3.
Check communication, transponder, TCAS and radio navigation settings.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

(h) Retained Minimum Equipment List (MEL) Revision, With No Changes

    This paragraph restates the requirements of paragraph (h) of AD 
2025-09-07, with no changes. Before further flight after 
accomplishing the requirements of paragraph (g) of this AD, revise 
the existing FAA-approved MEL to prohibit airplane dispatch 
(operation) with an inoperative RMP 3 under the MEL item equivalent 
to FAA master minimum equipment list 23-81-01.

[[Page 66019]]

(i) Retained Credit for Previous Actions, With No Changes

    This paragraph restates the credit specified in paragraph (i) of 
AD 2025-09-07, with no changes.
    (1) This paragraph provides credit for the AFM revision required 
by paragraph (g) of this AD, if the revision was performed before 
May 15, 2025 (the effective date of AD 2025-09-07) using Airbus 
A330/A340 Operations Engineering Bulletin 58, issue 1.0, dated 
February 7, 2025.
    (2) This paragraph provides credit for the AFM revision required 
by paragraph (g) of this AD, if the revision was performed before 
May 15, 2025 (the effective date of AD 2025-09-07) using Airbus A330 
Airplane Flight Manual Temporary Revision TR819, Issue 1, dated 
March 6, 2025.

(j) New Requirements

    Except as specified in paragraph (k) of this AD: Comply with all 
required actions and compliance times specified in, and in 
accordance with, EASA AD 2025-0286. Doing the modification required 
by this paragraph terminates the requirements of paragraphs (g) and 
(h) of this AD. After the modification has been done, the AFM 
revision required by paragraph (g) of this AD may be removed from 
the AFM and the MEL revision required by paragraph (h) of this AD 
may be removed from the MEL.

(k) Exceptions to EASA AD 2025-0286

    (1) Where EASA AD 2025-0286 refers to its effective date, this 
AD requires using the effective date of this AD.
    (2) This AD does not adopt paragraphs (1), (2), and (3) of EASA 
AD 2025-0286.
    (3) This AD does not adopt the ``Remarks'' section of EASA AD 
2025-0286.
