SUMMARY: The FAA proposes to adopt a new airworthiness directive (AD) 
for all Airbus Helicopters Deutschland GmbH (AHD) Model MBB-BK 117 D-2 
helicopters. This proposed AD was prompted by certification test 
results of a certain common cargo hook beam (CCHB) on the affected 
helicopters, which revealed that the installed CCHB could result in 
fuel leakage following a survivable accident. This proposed AD would 
require modification of the CCHB and fuel system and installation of 
structural reinforcement. This proposed AD would also prohibit 
installing a certain CCHB on any helicopter. The FAA is proposing this 
AD to address the unsafe condition on these products.

Background

    EASA, which is the Technical Agent for the Member States of the 
European Union, has issued EASA AD 2025-0153, dated July 18, 2025 (EASA 
AD 2025-0153) (also referred to as the MCAI), to correct an unsafe 
condition on all AHD Model MBB-BK117 D-2 helicopters, all variants. The 
MCAI states that certification testing of the CCHB installation on AHD 
Model MBB-BK117 D-3 and D-3m helicopters revealed that a survivable 
accident could result in fuel leakage. The MCAI further states that, 
due to design similarity, AHD

[[Page 65493]]

Model MBB-BK117 D-2 helicopters, all variants, could also be affected 
by the same unsafe condition.
    This condition, if not addressed, could lead to fuel leakage and a 
possible fire after a survivable accident involving a helicopter with a 
certain CCHB installed.
    You may examine the MCAI in the AD docket at regulations.gov under 
Docket No. FAA-2026-11812.

Material Incorporated by Reference Under 1 CFR Part 51

    The FAA reviewed EASA AD 2025-0153, which specifies procedures for 
modification of the CCHB and the fuel system and installation of 
structural reinforcement. EASA AD 2025-0153 also specifies that, as an 
alternative to the modification, operation of a helicopter with an 
affected CCHB part number L851M1301101 installed is prohibited.
    Additionally, EASA AD 2025-0153 specifies procedures to modify the 
helicopter by installing an improved cargo hook weighing system 
concurrently with the modification. EASA AD 2025-0153 also specifies 
that installation of an affected CCHB on any helicopter is prohibited.
    This material is reasonably available because the interested 
parties have access to it through their normal course of business or by 
the means identified in the ADDRESSES section.

FAA's Determination

    These products have been approved by the civil aviation authority 
(CAA) of another country and are approved for operation in the United 
States. Pursuant to the FAA's bilateral agreement with this State of 
Design Authority, that authority has notified the FAA of the unsafe 
condition described in the MCAI referenced above. The FAA is issuing 
this NPRM after determining that the unsafe condition described 
previously is likely to exist or develop on other products of the same 
type design.

Proposed AD Requirements in This NPRM

    This proposed AD would require accomplishing the actions specified 
in EASA AD 2025-0153, described previously, as incorporated by 
reference, except for any differences identified as exceptions in the 
regulatory text of this proposed AD.

Explanation of Required Compliance Information

    In the FAA's ongoing efforts to improve the efficiency of the AD 
process, the FAA developed a process to use some CAA ADs as the primary 
source of information for compliance with requirements for 
corresponding FAA ADs. The FAA has been coordinating this process with 
manufacturers and CAAs. As a result, the FAA proposes to incorporate 
EASA AD 2025-0153 by reference in the FAA final rule. This proposed AD 
would, therefore, require compliance with EASA AD 2025-0153 in its 
entirety through that incorporation, except for any differences 
identified as exceptions in the regulatory text of this proposed AD. 
Using common terms that are the same as the heading of a particular 
section in EASA AD 2025-0153 does not mean that operators need comply 
only with that section. For example, where the AD requirement refers to 
``all required actions and compliance times,'' compliance with this AD 
requirement is not limited to the section titled ``Required Action(s) 
and Compliance Time(s)'' in EASA AD 2025-0153. Material required by 
EASA AD 2025-0153 for compliance will be available at regulations.gov 
under Docket No. FAA-2026-11812 after the FAA final rule is published.

Authority for This Rulemaking

    Title 49 of the United States Code specifies the FAA's authority to 
issue rules on aviation safety. Subtitle I, section 106, describes the 
authority of the FAA Administrator. Subtitle VII: Aviation Programs, 
describes in more detail the scope of the Agency's authority.
    The FAA is issuing this rulemaking under the authority described in 
Subtitle VII, Part A, Subpart III, Section 44701: General requirements. 
Under that section, Congress charges the FAA with promoting safe flight 
of civil aircraft in air commerce by prescribing regulations for 
practices, methods, and procedures the Administrator finds necessary 
for safety in air commerce. This regulation is within the scope of that 
authority because it addresses an unsafe condition that is likely to 
exist or develop on products identified in this rulemaking action.

Airbus Helicopters Deutschland GmbH (AHD): Docket No. FAA-2026-
11812; Project Identifier MCAI-2025-01222-R.

(a) Comments Due Date

    The FAA must receive comments on this airworthiness directive 
(AD) by November 30, 2026.

(b) Affected ADs

    None.

(c) Applicability

    This AD applies to all Airbus Helicopters Deutschland GmbH (AHD) 
Model MBB-BK 117 D-2 helicopters, certificated in any category.

(d) Subject

    Joint Aircraft System Component (JASC) Code 2500, Cabin 
equipment/furnishings.

(e) Unsafe Condition

    This AD was prompted by certification test results of the 
installation of a certain common cargo hook beam (CCHB) identifying 
that the installed CCHB could result in fuel leakage following a 
survivable accident. The FAA is issuing this AD to prevent possible 
fuel leakage. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could lead to 
fuel leakage and a possible fire after a survivable accident 
involving a helicopter equipped with a certain CCHB.

(f) Compliance

    Comply with this AD within the compliance times specified, 
unless already done.

(g) Required Actions

    Except as specified in paragraphs (h) and (i) of this AD: Comply 
with all required actions and compliance times specified in, and in 
accordance with, European Union Aviation Safety Agency AD 2025-0153, 
dated July 18, 2025 (EASA AD 2025-0153).

(h) Exceptions to EASA AD 2025-0153

    (1) Where EASA AD 2025-0153 refers to its effective date, this 
AD requires using the effective date of this AD.
    (2) Where EASA AD 2025-0153 requires compliance in terms of 
flight hours, this AD requires using hours time-in-service.
    (3) Where the material referenced in EASA AD 2025-0153 specifies 
``discard'', this AD requires replacing that text with ``remove from 
service''.
    (4) Where the material referenced in EASA AD 2025-0153 specifies 
``check'', this AD requires replacing that text with ``inspect''.
    (5) This AD does not adopt the ``Remarks'' section of EASA AD 
2025-0153.

(i) No Reporting Requirement

    Although the material referenced in EASA AD 2025-0153 specifies 
to submit information to the manufacturer, this AD does not require 
that action.
