AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA proposes to adopt a new airworthiness directive (AD) 
for certain Baykar Piaggio Aerospace S.p.A. (Piaggio) Model P-180 
airplanes. This proposed AD was prompted by a report of corrosion and 
cracks found around the head of attaching fasteners on main wing lower 
skin external reinforcement doublers. This proposed AD would require 
repetitive high frequency eddy current (HFEC) inspections of the lower 
skin reinforcement and, depending on findings, corrective action. The 
FAA is proposing this AD to address the unsafe condition on these 
products.

Background

    The European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA), which is the 
Technical Agent for the Member States of the European Union, has issued 
EASA AD 2024-0006, dated January 9, 2024 (EASA AD 2024-0006) (also 
referred to as the MCAI), to correct an unsafe condition on certain 
Piaggio Model P-180 airplanes. The MCAI states a report of an 
occurrence of corrosion and cracks

[[Page 65145]]

was found around the heads of attaching fasteners on main wing lower 
skin external reinforcement doublers during scheduled maintenance on a 
Piaggio Model P-180 airplane. This condition, if not detected and 
corrected, could reduce the structural integrity of the main wing with 
consequent reduced control of the airplane. To address this unsafe 
condition, Piaggio issued material providing instructions for 
repetitive HFEC inspections of the affected parts and, depending on 
findings, accomplishment of corrective action.
    You may examine the MCAI in the AD docket at regulations.gov under 
Docket No. FAA-2026-11809.

Material Incorporated by Reference Under 1 CFR Part 51

    The FAA reviewed Piaggio Aerospace Service Bulletin 80-0494 Rev. 1, 
dated November 30, 2023. This material specifies procedures for 
repetitive HFEC inspections on the main wing lower skin external 
reinforcement doublers, and corrective actions for the parts with 
evidence of corrosion and cracks. Corrective actions include, depending 
on inspection findings, repairing or contacting Piaggio to obtain an 
approved repair design approval sheet.
    The FAA also reviewed Piaggio Aerospace Service Bulletin 80-0482 
Rev. 13, dated January 27, 2026. This material specifies weight and 
balance updates and identifies updates to the effectivity of 
manufacturer's serial numbers affected by the weight and balance 
updates.
    The FAA also reviewed Subject 51-10-40, Allowable Damage, Piaggio 
Aerospace Structural Repair Manual, Report No. 180-MAN-0250-1106, Issue 
D0, dated November 2, 2021. This material specifies identifying what is 
considered allowable minor damage (not critical to affect flight safety 
or performance) to primary and secondary structures that may be 
corrected by a simple procedure.
    This material is reasonably available because the interested 
parties have access to it through their normal course of business or by 
the means identified in the ADDRESSES section.

FAA's Determination

    These products have been approved by the civil aviation authority 
of another country and are approved for operation in the United States. 
Pursuant to the FAA's bilateral agreement with this State of Design 
Authority, that authority has notified the FAA of the unsafe condition 
described in the MCAI and material referenced above. The FAA is issuing 
this NPRM after determining that the unsafe condition described 
previously is likely to exist or develop on other products of the same 
type design.

Proposed AD Requirements in This NPRM

    This proposed AD would require accomplishing the actions specified 
in the material already described.

Authority for This Rulemaking

    Title 49 of the United States Code specifies the FAA's authority to 
issue rules on aviation safety. Subtitle I, section 106, describes the 
authority of the FAA Administrator. Subtitle VII: Aviation Programs, 
describes in more detail the scope of the Agency's authority.
    The FAA is issuing this rulemaking under the authority described in 
Subtitle VII, Part A, Subpart III, Section 44701: General requirements. 
Under that section, Congress charges the FAA with promoting safe flight 
of civil aircraft in air commerce by prescribing regulations for 
practices, methods, and procedures the Administrator finds necessary 
for safety in air commerce. This regulation is within the scope of that 
authority because it addresses an unsafe condition that is likely to 
exist or develop on products identified in this rulemaking action.

Baykar Piaggio Aerospace S.p.A. (Type Certificate Previously Held by 
Piaggio Aviation S.p.A.): Docket No. FAA-2026-11809; Project 
Identifier MCAI-2024-00028-A.

(a) Comments Due Date

    The FAA must receive comments on this airworthiness directive 
(AD) by November 30, 2026.

(b) Affected ADs

    None.

(c) Applicability

    This AD applies to Baykar Piaggio Aerospace S.p.A. (type 
certificate previously held by Piaggio Aviation S.p.A.) (Piaggio) 
Model P-180 airplanes, certificated in any category, manufacturer 
serial numbers 1032, 1036, 1037, 1039, 1040, 1043, 1063, 1065, 1074, 
1079, 1089, 1106, 1108 and 1112.

(d) Subject

    Joint Aircraft System Component (JASC) Code 5730, Wing, Plates/
Skins.

(e) Unsafe Condition

    This AD was prompted by a report of corrosion and cracks found 
around the heads of attaching fasteners on main wing lower skin 
external reinforcement doublers. The FAA is issuing this AD to 
address this unsafe condition. The unsafe condition, if not 
addressed, could reduce the structural integrity of the main wing 
with reduced control of the airplane.

(f) Compliance

    Comply with this AD within the compliance times specified, 
unless already done.

(g) Definitions

    For the purpose of this AD:
    (1) An ``affected part'' is a main wing lower skin external 
reinforcement doubler, having part number (P/N) 80-212457-001 (left-
hand) or P/N 80-212457-002 (right-hand).
    (2) The ``material'' is Piaggio Aerospace Service Bulletin (SB) 
80-0494 Rev. 1, dated November 30, 2023 (Piaggio SB 80-0494 Rev. 1).
    (3) ``Group 1'' airplanes are those on which, on the effective 
date of this AD, Piaggio Aerospace Service Bulletin 80-0482 Rev.13, 
dated January 27, 2026 (Piaggio SB 80-0482 Rev. 13) is not embodied, 
but on which, before the effective date of this AD, Piaggio SB 80-
0482 Rev. 13 has been embodied and later removed.
    (4) ``Group 2'' airplanes are those on which, on the effective 
date of this AD, Piaggio SB 80-0482 Rev. 13 is embodied.
    (5) ``Group 3'' airplanes are those on which, on the effective 
date of this AD, Piaggio SB 80-0482 Rev. 13 has never been embodied.
    (6) The ``High Frequency Eddy Current (HFEC) inspection'' is for 
the affected parts (lower skin reinforcement) of an airplane in 
accordance with the instructions in Section 2.B.(13) of Piaggio SB 
80-0494 Rev. 1.
    (7) ``Repairable Corrosion'' is corrosion identified as 
``repairable'' in Subject 51-10-40, Allowable Damage, Piaggio 
Aerospace Structural Repair Manual, Report No. 180-MAN-0250-1106, 
Issue D0, dated November 2, 2021, except corrosion around the heads 
of the attaching fasteners of affected parts.

compliance time and, thereafter, at intervals not to exceed the 
values as defined in table 1 of this AD, as applicable, accomplish

    (2) If, during any inspection required by paragraph (h)(1) of 
this AD, repairable corrosion, as defined in this AD, is detected, 
before next flight, accomplish a repair in accordance with the 
instructions of steps 2.B.(15) through (19), and C.(20) of Piaggio 
SB 80-0494 Rev. 1.
    (3) If, during any inspection required by paragraph (h)(1) of 
this AD, any discrepancy other than repairable corrosion is detected 
on an airplane, before next flight, contact either the Manager, 
International Validation Branch, FAA; European Union Aviation Safety 
Agency (EASA); or Piaggio's EASA Design Organization Approval (DOA). 
If approved by the DOA, the approval must include the DOA-authorized 
signature and, within the compliance time defined therein, 
accomplish that repair accordingly, including any post-repair 
follow-on action(s), as applicable.
    (4) Accomplishment of corrective actions on an airplane, as 
required by paragraph (h)(2) or (3) of this AD, as applicable, does 
not constitute terminating action for the repetitive inspections 
required by paragraph (h)(1) of this AD for that airplane, unless 
otherwise specified in the approved repair instructions.
    (5) For Group 1 airplanes: From the effective date of this AD, 
it is allowed to embody Piaggio SB 80-0482 Rev. 13, on an airplane, 
provided that, before next flight after that modification, an HFEC 
inspection, as defined in this AD, and, depending on findings, 
corrective action(s) as required by paragraph (h)(2) or (3) of this 
AD, as applicable, are accomplished on that airplane. After that 
modification, the airplane is effectively considered to be in Group 
2, and HFEC inspections must be accomplished on that airplane as 
required by this AD for Group 2 airplanes.
    (6) For Group 2 airplanes: From the effective date of this AD, 
following removal of Piaggio SB 80-0482 Rev. 13 from an airplane, 
that airplane is effectively considered to be in Group 1, and HFEC 
inspections must be accomplished on that airplane as required by 
this AD for Group 1 airplanes.
    (7) For Group 3 airplanes: From the effective date of this AD, 
it is allowed to embody Piaggio SB 80-0482 Rev. 13 on an airplane, 
provided that, within 660 hours TIS or 25 months after that 
modification, whichever occurs first, an HFEC inspection, as defined 
in this AD, and, depending on findings, corrective action(s) as 
required by paragraph (h)(2) or (3) of this AD, as applicable, are 
accomplished on that airplane. After that modification, the airplane 
is effectively considered to be in Group 2, and HFEC inspections 
must be accomplished on that airplane as required by this AD for 
Group 2 airplanes.
