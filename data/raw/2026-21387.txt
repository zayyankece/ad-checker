Airworthiness Directives; Airbus Helicopters

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The FAA proposes to adopt a new airworthiness directive (AD) 
for all Airbus Helicopters Model AS350B3, AS355F, AS355F1, AS355F2, 
AS355N, AS355NP, EC130B4, and EC130T2 helicopters. This proposed AD was 
prompted by a report of the loss of the tightening torque between the 
upper ball bearing end and a main rotor (MR) servo-control. This 
proposed AD would require an inspection of the nut tightening torque of 
the upper ball bearing end of all the MR servo-controls and, depending 
on the results, corrective actions. The proposed AD would also require 
reporting the inspection results. The FAA is proposing this AD to 
address the unsafe condition on these products.

Background

    EASA, which is the Technical Agent for the Member States of the 
European Union, has issued EASA AD 2026-0132, dated July 8, 2026 (EASA 
AD 2026-0132) (also referred to as the MCAI), to correct an unsafe 
condition on all Airbus Helicopters Model AS 350 B3, AS 355 F, AS 355 
F1, AS 355 F2, AS 355 N, AS 355 NP, EC 130 B4, and EC 130 T2 
helicopters. The MCAI states a report was received of the loss of the 
tightening torque between the upper ball bearing end and an MR servo-
control. The MCAI also states further investigations determined that 
the presence of paint or protection on the nut retainer (lock-washer) 
is the root cause for this loss of tightening torque, and that this 
deficiency may occur on any of the three (forward, left, and right) MR 
servo-controls installed on a helicopter.
    The FAA is issuing this proposed AD to inspect the nut tightening 
torque of the upper ball bearing end of all the MR servo-controls and, 
if necessary, accomplish corrective actions. The unsafe condition, if 
not addressed, could result in the disconnection of the upper ball 
bearing end from the MR servo-control and loss of control of a 
helicopter.
    You may examine the MCAI in the AD docket at regulations.gov under 
Docket No. FAA-2026-11814.

Material Incorporated by Reference Under 1 CFR Part 51

    The FAA reviewed EASA AD 2026-0132, which specifies procedures for 
a one-time inspection and thereafter, after each removal and 
installation of an MR servo-control, inspection of the nut tightening 
torque of the upper ball bearing end of the forward, left, and right MR 
servo-controls, having a part number (P/N) and manufacturer P/N as 
identified in Appendix 1 of the MCAI, and the associated nut retainer 
(lock-washer) having manufacturer P/N 360A27-2121-20 or 365A27-8193-20. 
Depending on the results, the MCAI specifies procedures for re-
inspecting the nut tightening torque and accomplishing corrective 
actions, including inspecting the threads of the MR servo-control and, 
if necessary, cleaning them; replacing the ball bearing end; replacing 
the MR servo-control; or contacting AH [Airbus Helicopters] for 
approved instructions. EASA AD 2026-0132 also requires reporting of all 
inspection results.
    This material is reasonably available because the interested 
parties have access to it through their normal course of business or by 
the means identified in the ADDRESSES section.

FAA's Determination

    These products have been approved by the civil aviation authority 
(CAA) of another country and are approved for operation in the United 
States. Pursuant to the FAA's bilateral agreement with this State of 
Design Authority, that authority has notified the FAA of the unsafe 
condition described in the MCAI referenced above. The FAA is issuing 
this NPRM after determining that the unsafe condition described 
previously is likely to exist or develop on other products of the same 
type design.

Proposed AD Requirements in This NPRM

    This proposed AD would require accomplishing the actions specified 
in EASA AD 2026-0132, described previously, as incorporated by 
reference, except for any differences identified as exceptions in the 
regulatory text of this AD. See ``Differences Between this Proposed AD 
and the MCAI'' for a discussion of the general differences included in 
this proposed AD.

Differences Between This Proposed AD and the MCAI

    Where the MCAI specifies contacting Airbus Helicopters if an 
unacceptable value of the nut tightening torque is found, this proposed 
AD would require performing corrective actions in accordance with a 
method approved by the Manager, International Validation Branch, FAA; 
or EASA; or Airbus Helicopters' Design Organization Approval (DOA). If 
approved by the DOA, the approval must include the DOA-authorized 
signature.

Authority for This Rulemaking

    Title 49 of the United States Code specifies the FAA's authority to 
issue rules on aviation safety. Subtitle I, section 106, describes the 
authority of the FAA Administrator. Subtitle VII: Aviation Programs, 
describes in more detail the scope of the Agency's authority.
    The FAA is issuing this rulemaking under the authority described in 
Subtitle VII, Part A, Subpart III, Section 44701: General requirements. 
Under that section, Congress charges the FAA with promoting safe flight 
of civil aircraft in air commerce by prescribing regulations for 
practices, methods, and procedures the Administrator finds necessary 
for safety in air commerce. This regulation is within the scope of that 
authority because it addresses an unsafe condition that is likely to 
exist or develop on products identified in this rulemaking action.

Airbus Helicopters: Docket No. FAA-2026-11814; Project Identifier 
MCAI-2026-00715-R.

(a) Comments Due Date

    The FAA must receive comments on this airworthiness directive 
(AD) by December 3, 2026.

(b) Affected ADs

    None.

(c) Applicability

    This AD applies to all Airbus Helicopters Model AS350B3, AS355F, 
AS355F1, AS355F2, AS355N, AS355NP, EC130B4, and EC130T2 helicopters, 
certificated in any category.

(d) Subject

    Joint Aircraft System Component (JASC) Code 6710, Main rotor 
control.

(e) Unsafe Condition

    This AD was prompted by a report of the loss of the tightening 
torque between the upper ball bearing end and a main rotor (MR) 
servo-control. The FAA is issuing this AD to inspect the nut 
tightening torque of the upper ball bearing end of all the MR servo-
controls and, if necessary, accomplish corrective actions. The 
unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in the 
disconnection of the upper ball bearing end from the MR servo-
control and loss of control of a helicopter.

(f) Compliance

    Comply with this AD within the compliance times specified, 
unless already done.

(g) Required Actions

    Except as specified in paragraph (h) of this AD: Comply with all 
required actions and compliance times specified in, and in 
accordance with European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) AD 
2026-0132, dated July 8, 2026 (EASA AD 2026-0132).

(h) Exceptions to EASA AD 2026-0132

    (1) Where EASA AD 2026-0132 refers to its effective date, this 
AD requires using the effective date of this AD.
    (2) Where EASA AD 2026-0132 refers to flight hours, this AD 
requires using hours time-in-service.
    (3) Where paragraph (5) of EASA AD 2026-0132 and the material 
referenced in EASA AD 2026-0132 specify to contact AH [Airbus 
Helicopters] for corrective actions and instructions, this AD 
requires accomplishing corrective actions in accordance with a 
method approved by the Manager, International Validation Branch, 
FAA; or EASA; or Airbus Helicopters' Design Organization Approval 
(DOA). If approved by the DOA, the approval must include the DOA-
authorized signature.
    (4) Where the material referenced in EASA AD 2026-0132 specifies 
discarding certain parts, this AD requires removing those parts from 
service.
    (5) Where the material referenced in EASA AD 2026-0132 specifies 
``check'', this AD requires replacing that text with ``inspection''.
    (6) This AD does not adopt the ``Remarks'' section of EASA AD 
2026-0132.

(i) Credit for Previous Actions

    This paragraph provides credit for the actions required by 
paragraph (g) of this AD, if those actions were performed before the 
effective date of this AD using the following:
    (1) Airbus Service Bulletin AS350-67-30-0002, dated 01 April 
2026.
    (2) Airbus Service Bulletin AS355-67-30-0001, dated 01 April 
2026.
    (3) Airbus Service Bulletin EC130-67-30-0001, dated 01 April 
2026.
